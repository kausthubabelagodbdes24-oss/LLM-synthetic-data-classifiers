## Limitations

- One seed (13) and one run per key: no standard deviation, confidence interval, significance test or stability claim is possible, and none is made. Differences of a few hundredths of macro-F1 can come from sampling alone.
- Small data: 30 training examples per label (180 per task) in C1-C4, 10 real sources per label in C5a.
- C5a (60 rows) and C5b (420 rows) intentionally differ in training size; C5b - C5a mixes the effect of AugGPT content with the effect of more rows.
- Task confounding: TREC and Emotion differ in more than subjectivity (label semantics, text length and style, test-set size 500 vs 2,000 and label balance). A cross-task difference in gaps is not attributable to subjectivity alone.
- Labelling costs differ: C3/C4 synthetic rows were generated from real few-shot demonstrations and C5b from real sources; these real examples are disclosed separately from classifier training counts.
- Synthetic-data quality was screened by model-assisted and heuristic review (issue #11), not by independent human annotators; known flagged AugGPT rows are retained as disclosed in the freeze manifest.
- TREC C1 and C4 contain one disclosed official train/test duplicate (trec.train.05262 = trec.test.00378), kept by lead decision on issue #13.
