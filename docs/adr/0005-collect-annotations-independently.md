# Collect annotations separately before combining responses

Historical record: [ADR0012](0012-eod-exploratory-30-seed13.md) supersedes conflicting size, repetition, human-annotation, audit-count and analysis requirements below. Unchanged model/source/integrity safeguards remain.


On 29 October 2026, Kausthuba approved replacing the shared answer sheet with separate response files for the same 60 items. Each annotator submits without seeing the others' responses or gold answers, and Hementh completes his own judgments before combining submissions for scoring; this supersedes the manuals' shared-column editing process. The extra collection step reduces opportunities for responses to influence one another while retaining the common item set and three-annotator agreement design.
