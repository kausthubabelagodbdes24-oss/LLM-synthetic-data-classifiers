# Audit generated content before the synthetic-data freeze

On 29 October 2026, Kausthuba approved a Checkpoint 2 audit of ten randomly selected examples from each task, generation setting and repetition: 180 examples across two tasks, three settings and three repetitions. Record label fit, naturalness, errors and ambiguities, and compare AugGPT examples with their source meanings; investigate recurring problems before freezing. The extra review complements exact-count checks and is a quality screen, not a population accuracy guarantee.
